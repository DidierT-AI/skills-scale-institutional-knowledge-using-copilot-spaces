# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, design test strategies, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria and quality benchmarks before release.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Define quality standards and testing scope for each release
- Execute manual and automated testing; coordinate test coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Report quality metrics and identify product defects
- Collaborate with developers on test automation and CI/CD integration
- Escalate quality risks and blockers to PM and Project Manager

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards before release
- Reduce production defects and improve customer experience
- Enable fast, confident releases through comprehensive testing

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions during sprint planning
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Acceptance criteria reviews and test case walkthroughs
- Escalations and risk communications

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business direction, approve project charters and funding, and remain informed of progress and risks that may impact organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation and provide business context
- Define success metrics and business outcomes
- Make trade-off decisions on scope, timeline, and budget
- Receive regular status updates and escalations
- Champion the project and ensure cross-organizational alignment
- Approve release decisions and major scope changes

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and align with strategy
- Make informed decisions on resource allocation
- Maintain visibility and accountability for project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates and dashboards
- Escalations for risks and decisions
- Release announcements and post-project reviews

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical guidance, make architectural decisions, and ensure the solution is robust, scalable, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Guide implementation approach and technology choices
- Conduct design reviews and mentor developers
- Ensure consistency with system standards and best practices
- Collaborate on estimation and feasibility assessments

### Goals
- Deliver a robust, scalable, and maintainable solution
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Share knowledge and build team capability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code and design reviews
- Technical risk assessments and escalations
- Planning and estimation collaboration

---

## UX/Product Designer

### Role Summary
Designers collaborate with product and development teams to create user-centered, intuitive, and visually cohesive experiences that delight customers.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design user interfaces and interaction flows
- Collaborate with Product Manager on feature requirements
- Provide design specs and prototypes to developers
- Iterate on design based on feedback and testing
- Ensure consistency with design system and brand guidelines

### Goals
- Maximize usability and customer satisfaction
- Reduce friction in the user experience
- Build consistent, delightful products

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Acceptance criteria and design handoff meetings
- Collaboration on feature prioritization

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and release processes to ensure reliable, secure, and efficient delivery to production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure and deployment environments
- Coordinate release processes and rollout planning
- Monitor system health, performance, and security
- Manage rollbacks and incident response
- Automate testing, security scanning, and deployment steps

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and repeatable releases
- Maintain high system availability and security
- Reduce deployment risk and manual effort

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and coordination meetings
- Deployment runbooks and incident playbooks
- Infrastructure and security updates
- Performance and reliability metrics

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and help teams continuously improve their delivery practices and collaboration.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and escalate impediments
- Coach team on agile principles and practices
- Track velocity and team metrics
- Drive continuous improvement and process refinement
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver predictable value
- Improve team velocity and collaboration
- Build a culture of continuous learning and improvement

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremonies and facilitation
- Impediment tracking and escalations
- Velocity and metrics reporting
- Retrospective action items and follow-up
